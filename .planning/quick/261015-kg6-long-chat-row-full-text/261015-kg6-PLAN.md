# Quick 261015-kg6: long chat rows show their full text

**Symptom:** a long assistant row ends at "…the lead-in text fix is merged into your f".

**Root cause (verified against serve):** the block is 2070 chars; "your f" ends at exactly char 2000.
`proseAttributed` truncates prose to `s.prefix(2000)` (Phase 5 T-05-02 DoS cap), silently. Not the
ev9 fit cache (7212ab7), which is cleared whenever `current` changes and keyed by width/lines/mode.

## Tasks

1. `proseAttributed`: raise the cap to 20 000 chars (still bounds a hostile payload) and end a cut text
   with "…" so truncation is never silent. Update the Phase 5 unit check.

   `OrchestratorSelectableText` in an NSHostingView at the open (4-line) and expanded widths; with a

   last glyph is laid out (not clipped), then again after the text grows (streaming) — guards the fit cache.
3. Re-run unit harness, run.sh, `--idle` (-Onone and -O) and compare typing/tick with ev9; rebuild Debug
